# Stakeholder interview guide (ISEF items 33, 202, 204) — IRB approval and consent FIRST

Audience: district water-quality officer, PHED chemist, panchayat member, health worker, well owner. 5 interviews, 20 minutes each.
No names recorded unless consent allows; record role, district, date. Questions are open; do not lead.

1. How is arsenic in drinking water tested here today? Who does it, how often, how long until the result comes back?
2. What happens when a result is above the limit? Who is told, how, and what do people do?
3. What would make a screening result trustworthy to you? What would make you ignore it?
4. If a device said "arsenic detected, confirm in a laboratory, do not drink", what would you do that day?
5. Who should own the device and the data? Who pays for strips and calibration?
6. What has gone wrong with past test kits or monitoring schemes?
7. (Show the result screen, item 203) What does this screen tell you? What would you do next? What is unclear?

Record verbatim one sentence per interview that you may quote (with permission). Summarise themes in the logbook.
