# Abstract structure (ISEF item 227) — 250 words, written by the student only

1. Purpose (1–2 sentences): the need with its number and source; the question.
2. Procedure (3–4 sentences): what was built, how it was validated (standards, blanks, paired laboratory samples, blinding, randomisation), how many samples and sites.
3. Results (3–4 sentences): the numbers with intervals — LOD, agreement with the laboratory (Deming slope, Bland–Altman bias), sensitivity at 10 µg/L with CI, uptime.
4. Conclusion (2 sentences): what the results do and do not show; what comes next.

Rules: no citations, no acknowledgements, no claims not in the results, past tense, no "AI-powered".
