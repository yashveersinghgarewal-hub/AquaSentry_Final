MANDATORY DISCLOSURE

Autonomous Screening Scope: AquaSentry is an autonomous environmental screening and surveillance tool designed for rapid spatial prioritization. It is not an ISO-17025 accredited certified laboratory instrument.

Method Detection Limit (LOD) vs. WHO Guideline:
The sensor's analytical Limit of Detection (LOD) is 0.020 mg/L (20 µg/L), while the World Health Organization (WHO) provisional drinking water guideline is 0.010 mg/L (10 µg/L). Because the sensor LOD exceeds the WHO guideline, a reading below 20 µg/L cannot mathematically rule out contamination between 10–20 µg/L. Certified laboratory testing (e.g. ICP-MS) is mandatory before declaring water safe for human consumption.

Conservative Precedence Guardrail: Deterministic public health thresholds strictly override model inference. AquaNet-V1 machine learning predictions can NEVER downgrade an analytical reading exceeding the India BIS 10500 limit (> 50 µg/L) to "Safe".
