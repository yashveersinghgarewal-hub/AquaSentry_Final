04 · Distribution channels

Go-to-market and customer acquisition

A multi-tier strategy aligned with government water-quality programmes and institutional buyers.

Government / B2G

Jal Jeevan Mission (Har Ghar Jal)

The Ministry of Jal Shakti funds water-quality monitoring and field-test upgrades across rural supply schemes, with the mission extended to 2028. Objective, logged electronic results replace subjective colour strips.

Target: 1.0–1.5% of the serviceable market — ₹21–32 Cr in node deployments.
Commercial / B2B

Aquaculture, bottling & industrial intake

Fish hatcheries, shrimp farms and food plants lose yield to unmonitored heavy metals. Nodes give 24/7 intake monitoring for a fraction of lab-contractor fees.

Target: ₹18,000–24,000 annual service contracts per facility.
Urban housing

Housing societies & municipal storage

Apartment complexes around Pune, Mumbai and Delhi NCR draw borewell water with unverified arsenic. AquaSentry Mini watches the overhead tank.

Target: 25,000 urban nodes by year 3 (5 per society × 5,000 complexes).
NGO & philanthropic

Water NGOs & impact grants

Partnerships with water NGOs to deploy screening boats in the Bengal basin (West Bengal and Bangladesh), where arsenic exposure affects tens of millions.

Target: grant-funded bulk deployments with open data.

Institutional procurement routes

  • Jal Jeevan Mission: water-testing innovation listing for Village Water & Sanitation Committees.
  • GeM (Government e-Marketplace): listing as an environmental water-testing device for direct tender purchase.
  • CSR funds: industry, energy and mining companies meeting clean-water obligations in affected basins.
Technology moat

AquaNet-V3 edge neural network

Field kits suffer from chemical cross-interference and subjective colour reading. AquaSentry reads five sensors and runs a trained 5-16-12-4 network on the ESP32 for every scan, then applies fixed BIS/WHO safety rules that can only make a verdict stricter.

06 · Product roadmap

Modular expansion and future scope

The electrode, potentiostat, microfluidics and radio work independently, so new capabilities do not need a new vessel.

Consumer line

Household in-tank version (AquaSentry Mini)

The same sensing chain in a compact pod that hangs inside an overhead tank or sump and sends phone alerts before contaminated water is used.

Build ₹2,800 ($29) · Retail ₹4,000 ($42)
Deep hydrology

Lead-screw depth profiling

A motorised syringe takes in water to sink the node to 10 m, then pushes it out to resurface — mapping how arsenic changes with depth near bottom sediments.

Firmware + mechanical add-on module
Multi-pollutant

Swap strips for other heavy metals

The LM358 potentiostat and ADS1115 ADC can run the voltage ramps for lead, copper, chromium, nickel and zinc. Insert an analyte-specific strip — no circuit changes.
