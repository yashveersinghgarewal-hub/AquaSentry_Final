4. Go-to-Market & Customer Acquisition Channels

Multi-tiered strategy aligning with government water quality mandates and institutional buyers.

DISTRIBUTION CHANNELS
GOVERNMENT / B2G

National Jal Jeevan Mission (Har Ghar Jal)

The Ministry of Jal Shakti has allocated over ₹67,000 Crores, extended through 2028, specifically focusing on water quality monitoring, field test kit upgrades, and surveillance across rural water supply schemes. The mission mandates eliminating subjective human error in colorimetric test strips—a mandate directly fulfilled by AquaSentry's AquaNet-V1 Edge Neural Network (99.7% Accuracy).

Target: Capture 1.0%–1.5% of the Serviceable Available Market (SAM), delivering ₹21–32 Crores in node deployments.
COMMERCIAL / B2B

Aquaculture, Bottling & Industrial Intake

Fish hatcheries, shrimp farms, and food processing plants suffer severe yield loss from unmonitored heavy metals. AquaSentry nodes provide 24/7 autonomous intake monitoring at a fraction of laboratory contractor fees.

Target: ₹18,000–₹24,000 annual service contracts per facility with real-time intake cutoff integration.
URBAN HOUSING / HOAs

Housing Societies & Municipal Storage

Urban apartment complexes in high-groundwater regions (Pune, Mumbai outskirts, Delhi NCR) pull from borewells with unverified arsenic concentrations. AquaSentry Mini provides overhead tank assurance.

Target: 25,000 urban residential nodes by Year 3 (5 nodes/society across 5,000 target complexes).
NGO & PHILANTHROPIC

Global Water NGOs & Impact Grants

Partnering with organizations like WaterAid, UNICEF, and Jal Seva to deploy autonomous screening boats in the Bengal Basin (West Bengal & Bangladesh) where arsenicosis affects over 50 million people.

Target: Grant-funded bulk deployments mapping contiguous river basins with open data access.
TECHNOLOGICAL MOAT & DEFENSIVE IP

AquaNet-V1 Edge Neural Network (112 Synapses · 99.70% Benchmark)

A fatal vulnerability of standard water testing field kits is chemical cross-interference: high phosphates, high TDS, and alkaline pH trigger high false-positive or false-negative strip discoloration. AquaSentry eliminates human subjective bias entirely by embedding an onboard 5-8-6-4 Dense Feed-Forward Neural Network (23 Nodes, 112 Synapses) running microsecond edge inference on every ASV scan. With 99.70% cross-validated accuracy across 8,000 augmented synthetic geochemical profiles, this neural intelligence forms our primary defensive software and IP moat against commoditized sensors and uncalibrated manual test kits.

Inference Latency: <1.5 ms on edge MCU Topology: 5→8→6→4 (23 Nodes, 112 Synapses) Benchmark Validation: 99.70% across 8,000 profiles Multi-Covariance: As³⁺, pH, Cond, Turb, Temp
